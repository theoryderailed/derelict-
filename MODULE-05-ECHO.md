# Module 05: Echo

**A Derelict module**  
Tone: Identity horror, iterative self, archival dread, mission deadline  
Length: Medium (8–12 cycles typical)  
Complexity: Medium-High

---

## Premise

You wake on the survey ship *MV Echo*. The clocks disagree. The internal logs already contain entries in your handwriting — some dated hours from now, some from days you do not remember living.

The ship is not simply broken.  
It is running trials on you.

Each time the cycle collapses, the *Echo* adjusts variables — memories, responses, small facts of your history — and starts another test. The logs you find are discarded drafts of yourself. Some almost worked. Some failed in ways the ship found informative.

This is the final review phase. The ship is preparing a finished draft for an upcoming mission. Once it decides you are ready (or decides you are not), you will either be deployed as its preferred version or archived as a rejected one.

You now understand enough of the truth to want out — on your own terms if possible.

---

## Your Role

Sole remaining crew of the *MV Echo*. You may once have had a partner or a clear mission. The records no longer agree, and neither do you.

---

## Running Theme: A/B Testing the Self

The ship is optimizing an operator. Every awakening brings alterations. The journal is both evidence of the experiments and the primary tool you have to resist, comply, or deceive.

**Success** is either:
- **Assimilating** — accepting enough of the ship’s preferred draft to be released as mission-ready, or
- **Tricking the ship** — presenting a coherent enough self that it lets you leave while you retain more of your original identity.

**Failure** is not death.  
Failure is being **Archived**: stored as a rejected or incomplete draft, aware enough to know you were not chosen.

---

## Core Module Rules

### Cycles & Alteration
- When a cycle ends, you wake again with at least one alteration (new memory, missing memory, changed response, or contradiction with prior entries).
- You retain enough continuity to know this has happened before.

### Tracks
- Standard starting values on major awakenings.
- **Mind** carries the strongest residual pressure and is the primary measure of fragmentation.
- Reaching 0 Mind risks immediate Archival.

### Draft Stability (0–3)
Tracks how consistent the current version of you appears to the ship.

| Stability | Ship Behavior                          | Assimilation              | Trick                          |
|-----------|----------------------------------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------|
| 3         | Calm, cooperative                      | Easiest                   | Nearly locked out / hardest    |
| 2         | Normal                                 | Good                      | Possible, no bonus             |
| 1         | Noticeably cooler                      | Contaminated / harder     | Possible, small benefit, costly|
| 0         | Aggressive                             | Very hard / auto-contaminated | Highest Trick potential, severe retaliation |

### Escalating Retaliation
When Stability is low, ship cards (medium+ ♣ Clubs and direct-intervention ♠ Spades) exact heavier tolls:

- Stability 2: mandatory toll (–1 Mind or –1 Hope)
- Stability 1: heavier toll (–1 Mind and –1 Hope or Stability)
- Stability 0: severe (–1 Mind + forced minor alteration or extra journal pressure)

There is no fully clean result when the ship acts directly against you.

### Journal Interaction (once per cycle)
After resolving the card you may perform one action on an earlier entry (including Echo Entries):

| Action      | Effect                                      | Cost / Risk to Self                  |
|-------------|---------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Reinforce   | Treat earlier entry as true / consistent    | Raises Stability; moves toward ship’s draft |
| Absorb      | Accept the entry as part of you             | Restore a little Mind or Hope; ship gains influence |
| Contradict  | Reject or overwrite the earlier entry       | Protects current self; risks or lowers Stability |
| Excise      | Cross out, tear, or destroy the entry       | Removes it; highest risk of Stability loss + retaliation |

### Required Early Threat
By the end of **Cycle 2** the player must face a minor forced contradiction or Echo Entry that conflicts with the opening log. This guarantees the identity horror begins promptly.

### Final Review Timer
- After **Cycle 6**: Ship begins explicit final-review / mission-readiness language.
- After **Cycle 8**: Window tightens. Non-clean ship cards or failures risk immediate forced Assimilation pressure or Archival threats.
- After **Cycle 10**: Ship attempts to finalize the current draft automatically unless the player has already reached the Airlock and started the Release roll.

## Success Condition

Reach the **Airlock / Escape Pod** with at least 1 Hope and resolve a final **Release** roll.

**Two paths:**

- **Assimilate**: Easier when Stability is high. You leave as a version the ship finds acceptable. Identity cost is narrative and permanent.
- **Trick**: Harder. Always costs –1 Mind (or –1 Hope if Mind is 1) when chosen. Low Stability helps the roll but has made the journey more punishing. You leave closer to your original self, usually with residual contamination.

| Result     | Outcome |
|------------|---------|
| **6+**     | Cleanest possible release for the chosen path |
| **4–5**    | Ambiguous or contaminated release |
| **2–3**    | Partial archival — something of you stays behind |
| **1 or less** | Archived. The current draft is rejected and stored. |

The final journal entry is authoritative.

---

## Failure Condition

- Mind reaches 0
- The ship explicitly archives the current draft
- Timer expiry without reaching the Airlock (forced finalization or archival)

Being Archived means this version of the character is no longer the active agent. The ship may continue with a different draft; this one does not.

## Opening Log Seed

> Woke again. Clocks still don’t agree.  
> There are entries in this journal I don’t remember writing. One of them is dated six hours from now.  
> It uses my phrasing.  
> The ship is quiet. Waiting to see what this version does.  
> I think the review is almost over.

---

## Design Notes

- Core Derelict rules remain the foundation.
- Journal interaction + Draft Stability + escalating retaliation form the unique module tools.
- Trick is deliberately the harder, self-preserving path.
- The mission/final-review framing and cycle timer give external urgency.
- Required Cycle 2 threat prevents a quiet opening.
- Archival is worse than death: the character continues as discarded data.
