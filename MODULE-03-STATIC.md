# Module 03: Static

**A Derelict module**  
Tone: Signal horror, epistemic dread, weaponized hope  
Length: Medium session (9–14 cycles)  
Complexity: Medium (narrative-directed)

---

## Premise

You are the last living technician on deep-space relay station **KX-11**. For 47 years the station has broadcast a continuous distress signal. You do not remember starting it. You only know you have been alone with it for a very long time.

Three days ago, a voice answered.

They claim to be a recovery specialist on final approach. Their voice is calm, professional, and knows your name. They tell you to prepare the docking collar and not to touch the antenna.

The signal is older than you. It has had caretakers before. None of them left.

---

## Your Role

Last remaining technician and caretaker of KX-11. The records no longer agree on how many people were ever stationed here, or what happened to them.

---

## Running Theme: The Lure

The distress signal is not a cry for help. It is a lure. Something old uses the station to attract ships. When a vessel responds and approaches, it is unmade or consumed. The process leaves little wreckage — only silence and, eventually, another lonely human who keeps the transmitter running.

The current “rescuer” may be a real pilot already being altered by the signal, or a projection generated to keep you cooperative. In either case, the signal needs you (or someone like you) to stay alive and keep broadcasting.

You will not be told this outright. You will piece it together.

---

## Scenario Goal

Decide what to do with the signal and the approaching voice, then reach a final Determination.

Two coherent goals exist in tension:

- **Aid the rescuer / extract** — possible genuine rescue, but you may be delivering a new ship (and yourself) to the entity.
- **Shut down the Antenna Array** — break the lure and stop future ships from dying, but risk condemning yourself to die alone.

Both choices are rational once the pattern becomes clear.

## Ship Layout (Static)

```
        Antenna Array
              |
     Communications Hub
         /          \
Habitation Ring    Power Core
         \          /
      Observation Deck
              |
     Airlock / Docking Collar
```

**Starting Zone:** Habitation Ring

**Connections (two-way):**
- Habitation Ring ↔ Communications Hub
- Habitation Ring ↔ Power Core
- Habitation Ring ↔ Observation Deck
- Communications Hub ↔ Antenna Array
- Communications Hub ↔ Observation Deck
- Power Core ↔ Observation Deck
- Observation Deck ↔ Airlock / Docking Collar

**Critical Zones**
- **Antenna Array** — source of the signal. Shutting it down is difficult and immediately angers or distorts the rescuer’s voice.
- **Communications Hub** — strongest contact with the rescuer; also strongest signal corruption.
- **Airlock / Docking Collar** — extraction point.
- **Observation Deck** — optical confirmation (or denial) of the approaching vessel, at a cost.

Use standard Stable / Hazardous / Compromised rules.

## Special Module Tools

### Rescuer Track (0–5)
Advances when you successfully contact or assist the rescuer.  
Higher values make final extraction easier but increase Signal pressure.

### Signal Knowledge (0–3)
Advances when you find major evidence of the lure pattern (mainly through Hearts and Clubs).  
Higher knowledge makes the final Determination more informed and raises the emotional stakes of your choice.

### Suit Event Table (Static)

When you draw a card, resolve it normally (rank intensity, roll if 6+, preferred tracks).  
Then use this table to guide what the discovery or event actually is.

| Suit       | Meaning in Static                          | Typical Discoveries / Events |
|------------|--------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| **♠ Spades** | Threat / Interference                     | System resistance, signal pushback, hostile contact, the voice becoming sharp or layered |
| **♥ Hearts** | Human Traces / Previous Caretakers        | Old logs, personal effects, the repeating pattern of prior victims who also heard a voice |
| **♦ Diamonds** | Station Systems / Signal Infrastructure | Antenna behavior, power fluctuations tied to contact, data corruption, polite system lies |
| **♣ Clubs** | Cosmic / Signal Truth                     | Direct wrongness, identity bleed, optical distortions, evidence that the signal is a lure |

**Rank** controls intensity.  
**Roll** controls how well you handle the discovery and what it costs.

**Station Filter:** On medium+ Diamonds or Clubs, the station systems (or the rescuer) often offer a calm, technical, or reassuring explanation that does not match what you just experienced.

---

## Contacting the Rescuer

Once per cycle, after resolving the card and writing the log, you may attempt contact (no movement required).

Roll d6:
- **5–6**: Clear contact. Rescuer Track +1. Temporary relief or useful information.
- **3–4**: Contact succeeds but costs you (–1 Mind or a corrupted memory/log).
- **1–2**: The voice is wrong — layered, anticipatory, or repeats something you have not yet said. Rescuer Track still +1, but –1 Mind.

As Rescuer Track rises, the voice becomes more intimate and more insistent that you do not shut down the antenna.

---

## Success Condition

Reach the **Airlock / Docking Collar** *or* successfully shut down the **Antenna Array**, with at least 1 Hope remaining, then resolve the final **Determination** roll.

### Final Determination Roll

Modifiers:
- +1 if Mind ≥ 3  
- +1 if Hope ≥ 3  
- +1 if Rescuer Track ≥ 3  
- +1 if Signal Knowledge ≥ 2  
- –1 if Integrity ≤ 2  
- –1 if you fully shut down the Antenna Array

| Result     | Outcome |
|------------|---------|
| **6+**     | Cleanest possible resolution. Either a real extraction occurs, or you kill the signal and remain yourself. Your journal supports your reading of events. |
| **4–5**    | Ambiguous success. You leave or the signal dies, but contradictory evidence remains. You will never be fully certain. |
| **2–3**    | Contaminated outcome. Something leaves the station — you, the signal, or both. The distinction has blurred. |
| **1 or less** | The signal wins. Either there was never a rescuer, or nothing left of you is separate from the broadcast. |

The final log entry is authoritative. The rules do not override what the player writes.

---

## Failure Conditions

- Any track reaches 0
- Both Airlock and Antenna Array become Compromised while you still have no viable path
- Mind reaches 0 while Rescuer Track is high — you may lose the ability to distinguish your own thoughts from the voice

> The signal is still up. I stopped counting how long I’ve been answering it.

> Said they’re on approach. Told me to keep the docking collar ready and not to touch the antenna.

## Design Notes

- Core Derelict rules remain unchanged.
- The suit event table gives directed narrative without replacing player interpretation.
- The lure pattern is discoverable through Hearts and Clubs so the player has real reason to distrust the rescuer.
- Both helping and shutting down remain coherent, costly choices.
- The module never fully confirms or denies the rescuer’s reality; the player’s final log does.
