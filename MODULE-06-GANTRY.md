# Module 06: Gantry

**A Derelict module**  
Tone: Industrial cosmic horror, social conformity, Lovecraftian scale  
Length: Medium (9–14 cycles)  
Complexity: Medium

---

## Premise

You are a rigger / maintenance tech on the outer scaffold of **Project Gantry**, a colossal orbital structure that has been under construction longer than anyone still working on it can cleanly remember. The company stopped answering months ago. Supply drops are irregular. The structure is still growing.

Workers have begun walking off the edge into open space. They do not scream. They step off at the end of a shift or during a quiet moment, calm as if simply going off-duty. The remaining crew has developed a hard, quiet culture of not talking about it. Anyone who keeps looking into the dark between the spars, or who refuses the extra “alignment” work, becomes a problem the crew eventually resolves.

Something vast is using the Gantry as a frame. In the darkness beyond the work lights, things move that should not move in vacuum.

---

## Your Role

Scaffold rigger / exterior maintenance. You still go outside. Most of the remaining crew prefers interior shifts now.

---

## Running Theme: Fall in Line or See Too Much

The horror has two faces:
1. The cosmic structure and what moves in the dark around it.
2. The exhausted, hard-eyed people who have already decided that survival means not looking and not asking.

High acceptance by the crew makes daily life safer and some tasks easier. Low acceptance makes you a liability. Looking clearly at the Gantry’s true nature costs Mind and Hope, and the crew notices.

---

## Special Module Tools

### Standing (0–3)
Measures how much the remaining crew trusts / accepts you.

| Standing | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| 3        | Trusted. Easier movement, some social friction free, better information |
| 2        | Normal. Default state |
| 1        | Watched. Restricted access, conversations stop when you approach |
| 0        | Liability. “Accidents” become likely; crew may actively work against you |

Raising Standing usually requires visible conformity (not looking, not asking, taking the extra shifts).  
Lowering Standing happens when you are seen staring into the dark, asking about the Walkers, or refusing alignment work.

### What You See in the Dark (Narrative Table)
When a card or situation calls for cosmic exposure, roll d12 or choose by intensity:

1. A section of scaffold continues in a direction geometry does not allow. Work lights refuse to illuminate it.  
2. One of the Walkers stands on nothing in full harness, turns their helmet toward you, and nods.  
3. Stars behind the farthest spars have been replaced by dark apertures that pulse with the docking clamps.  
4. Your shadow on the spar is cast in three slightly different directions at once.  
5. A coworker’s face inside a helmet already drifting away. Calm expression. Mouth moving.  
6. Something enormous passes behind the structure. It takes nearly a full minute. No one on the channel speaks.  
7. The weld you just finished has tiny, perfect teeth marks along the bead.  
8. Local comms recite the safety checklist in reverse, then ask if you are still keeping count.  
9. Darkness between two spars is thicker than vacuum. Tools near it disappear without drifting.  
10. You see the entire Gantry from an impossible distance. It is curved around something not visible.  
11. Footprints in exterior frost — human boots, wrong stride, ending in open space.  
12. For a moment the structure is transparent and you understand what it is scaffolding. The understanding does not fit inside a human skull for long.

Higher rank cards produce closer, more personal, or more prolonged visions.

## Facility Layout (Static – Vertical Emphasis)

```
Upper Spars / Beacon Array
        |
Main Scaffold Hub ─── Crew Hab Modules
        |
Deep Spars / Growth Edge
        |
Exterior Airlock ─── Damaged Shuttle Dock
```

**Starting Zone:** Crew Hab Modules or Main Scaffold Hub  

**Critical Zones:**  
- Deep Spars / Growth Edge — strongest cosmic exposure  
- Exterior Airlock / Damaged Shuttle Dock — primary escape route  
- Upper Spars — long-range beacon (risky alternative)

---

## Tracks

Standard starting values.  
Mind is under heavy pressure from visions.  
Hope erodes from isolation and the normalization of the Walkers.  
Integrity suffers from the physical danger of the scaffold and “accidents.”

---

## Suit Guidance (Gantry)

| Suit       | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **♠ Spades** | Physical danger, accidents, crew hostility, direct threat |
| **♥ Hearts** | Crew social pressure, Walkers, personal cost of conformity or resistance |
| **♦ Diamonds** | Scaffold systems, company remnants, structural growth, equipment |
| **♣ Clubs** | Pure cosmic exposure, visions in the dark, the frame’s true nature |

---

## Success Condition

Reach the **Damaged Shuttle Dock** or successfully activate the **Upper Beacon** and survive long enough for extraction, with at least 1 Hope remaining.  

Final escape roll is modified by Standing (high Standing may help logistics but can mean the crew tries to stop you) and by how much cosmic knowledge you carry.

---

## Failure Conditions

- Any track reaches 0  
- Standing reaches 0 and the crew resolves the problem  
- You step off the edge (voluntary or otherwise)

## Opening Log Seed

> Another exterior shift. The company channel is still dead.  
> Two more empty harnesses on the rack this morning. No one mentioned them.  
> The deep spars are longer than they were last rotation. I measured.  
> When I said so in the hab, conversation stopped.  
> I go back out anyway. Someone has to check the welds.

---

## Design Notes

- Social pressure and cosmic exposure are the twin engines.  
- The narrative table delivers lush, specific Lovecraftian-industrial imagery.  
- Standing makes conformity mechanically attractive and resistance costly.  
- Distinct from all previous modules by being the only one with a living crew and strong social horror.
