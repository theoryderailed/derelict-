# Derelict — Full Rules

*A solo sci-fi / cosmic horror journaling game*

---

## Premise

You are alone on a damaged or abandoned vessel or station. Something is wrong with it — and it is getting worse. You keep a log while you try to survive, understand what happened, or simply hold on as long as you can.

---

## What You Need

- One standard deck of 52 playing cards (Jokers removed)
- One six-sided die (d6)
- A notebook, document, or voice recorder for your log

---

## Character

Write a short description (2–4 sentences maximum):
- Name
- Role / background
- How you ended up alone here

That is all. There are no other character statistics.

---

## The Three Tracks

All tracks start at these fixed values:

| Track         | Starting Value | Represents                                       |
|---------------|----------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| **Integrity** | 6              | Physical state of the ship/station and your gear |
| **Mind**      | 6              | Your psychological stability                     |
| **Hope**      | 4              | Your remaining belief that escape is possible    |

When any track reaches **0**, something permanent and bad happens (see *Ending the Game*).

---

## Setup

1. Create your character.
2. Set the three tracks to their starting values.
3. Shuffle the deck.
4. Write your opening log entry (how you arrived or woke up alone).

---

## The Core Loop

Each cycle represents a stretch of time (a few hours, a watch, or a day).

1. **Draw one card**  
   This is the main event of the cycle.

2. **Interpret the card** using the suit and rank guidance below.  
   Decide what specifically happens in the fiction.

3. **Resolve danger** (when needed)  
   Roll the d6 when the situation is risky or the card demands it:
   - **5–6**: Clean success
   - **3–4**: Success with a cost (usually –1 to one track)
   - **1–2**: Failure (usually –1 to two tracks, or –2 to one)

4. **Adjust tracks** according to the result and the intensity of the card.

5. **Write a log entry**  
   This is mandatory. Write in-character. The log *is* the game.

Then begin the next cycle.

---

## Card Meanings

### Suits

| Suit       | Meaning                  | Typical Pressure                            |
|------------|--------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| ♠ Spades   | Threat / Hostility       | Something aggressive or actively dangerous  |
| ♥ Hearts   | Human Traces / Memory    | Logs, bodies, personal effects, past events |
| ♦ Diamonds | Systems / Environment    | Failures, hull issues, life support, power  |
| ♣ Clubs    | The Unknown / Cosmic     | Impossible geometry, entities, reality breaks |

### Rank Intensity

- **2–5**: Low intensity. Subtle, deniable, or manageable.
- **6–10**: Medium intensity. Clear and costly.
- **Jack / Queen / King**: High intensity. Strong version of the suit.
- **Ace**: Major turning point. Something permanent changes about the situation or the vessel.

Higher ranks should feel harder to ignore or rationalize.

---

## Ending the Game

The game ends when any of the following occur:

- **Integrity reaches 0** — The structure or your equipment fails completely. You are trapped with no remaining resources.
- **Mind reaches 0** — You break. Future log entries (if any) become fragmented, strange, or written in a different voice.
- **Hope reaches 0** — You accept there is no escape. The remaining entries become quieter, resigned, or alien.
- You choose to end it (final escape attempt, confrontation, or deliberate shutdown of the log).

You may continue writing a few final entries after a track hits zero if it feels right for the story.

---

## Optional Light Rules

These are not required. Use only if you want extra texture.

- **Face-down draw**: When you draw a black card (♠ or ♣), you may draw the next card face-down and only reveal it after writing part of the log. This increases dread.
- **Burn a card**: Once per game you may permanently remove a card from the deck to cancel a catastrophic result. Doing so costs –1 Hope.
- **Rest cycle**: You may spend a cycle trying to recover (+1 to one track, maximum starting value). You still draw a card — the place does not stay quiet.

---

## Design Notes

- Starting track values are fixed for consistency and speed of setup.
- The system is intentionally lethal and tends toward tragic or unsettling endings.
- One short session (roughly 6–10 cycles) is a complete experience.
- Longer play is possible by treating each major section of the vessel as a new phase and carefully managing track recovery.
