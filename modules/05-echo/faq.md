# Echo — FAQ

**Q: What is Draft Stability?**  
A: A 0–3 measure of how consistent the current version of you appears to the ship. High Stability makes Assimilation easier and the ship calmer. Low Stability helps the Trick path but makes the ship more aggressive.

**Q: When does the ship exact a toll?**  
A: On medium+ Clubs and direct-intervention Spades. At low Stability the tolls escalate (mandatory loss, then heavier loss, then forced alterations).

**Q: What do the journal actions do?**  
A:  
- Reinforce / Absorb → raise Stability, move toward the ship’s preferred draft  
- Contradict / Excise → protect current self, risk or lower Stability, invite retaliation

**Q: Why is there a required threat by Cycle 2?**  
A: To guarantee the identity horror starts promptly instead of a quiet opening.

**Q: What is the difference between Assimilate and Trick?**  
A: Assimilate is easier when Stability is high but costs identity. Trick is harder, always costs –1 Mind at the end, and lets you leave closer to your original self (usually with residual contamination).
