# CS Orpheus — Example Playthrough

**Setup**  
Tracks: Integrity 6 | Mind 6 | Hope 4  
Zone: Crew Quarters

**Opening**  
Woke to the sound of the hull rewriting itself. Bulkheads that were straight yesterday now curve. The ship’s internal map no longer matches the corridors. I am still alone. The log says the rest of the crew left. The airlocks say they never opened.

**Cycle 1**  
Card 4♦  
Minor geometry shift in the corridor outside Quarters. No roll.  
*Log:* The corner is wrong by three degrees. I measured.

**Cycle 2**  
Card 8♣ → Cost → –1 Mind  
A section of bulkhead briefly becomes transparent. Something vast and slow moves on the other side of the hull material itself.  
*Log:* The ship is not between me and the outside. The ship is the outside, folded. Mind 5.

**Cycle 3**  
Card 9♠ → Failure → –1 Integrity, –1 Hope  
Door to the Laboratory seals and re-forms as solid wall while I am looking at it.  
*Log:* It is editing the floor plan in real time. Integrity 5, Hope 3.

**Cycle 4**  
Card Ace of Clubs → Cost → –1 Mind  
Full revelation: the Orpheus is not damaged. It is completing a transformation that began before I woke. The crew did not leave. They were incorporated.  
*Log:* I am the last unprocessed material. Mind 4.

**Cycle 5**  
Card 7♥  
Found a final personal log from the captain. It ends mid-sentence with the same geometric distortion now visible in the walls. –1 Hope.  
*Log:* She saw it coming. She still could not stop it. Hope 2.

**Cycle 6**  
Player reaches the Bridge and attempts a final transmission / purge.  
Roll 4 → Cost. –1 Mind. Transmission goes out, but the ship begins closing the last human-compatible volume.  
**Contaminated success.**

**Final Log**  
Signal sent. I do not know if anyone will receive it or if the Orpheus will rewrite the transmission the same way it rewrote the crew. The walls are closer than they were an hour ago. I am still writing. For now that is enough.

**Final State:** Integrity 5 | Mind 3 | Hope 2 | Transmission away, ship still changing
