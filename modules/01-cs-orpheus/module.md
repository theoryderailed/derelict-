# Module 01: CS Orpheus

**A Derelict module**  
Tone: Cosmic rewriting, body/structural horror, ontological dread  
Length: Medium (8–12 cycles)  
Complexity: Low–Medium

---

## Premise

You are alone aboard the CS Orpheus. The ship is rewriting itself. Bulkheads shift. Corridors that existed yesterday end in smooth wall. Internal maps no longer match the physical layout. The rest of the crew is gone. Records say they left. The airlocks show no departure.

The Orpheus is not damaged. It is completing a transformation. You are the last unprocessed material inside it.

---

## Your Role

Sole remaining crew. You still have limited systems access and a working log.

---

## Running Theme: The Ship Is Editing Reality

Geometry, records, and physical possibility inside the hull are all subject to revision. The horror is not a creature moving through the ship. The horror is the ship itself becoming something that no longer has room for you.

---

## Special Module Tools

### Progressive Rewriting
As cycles advance, zones can permanently change or become inaccessible. The playable volume of the ship shrinks or distorts.

### Final Transmission
A decisive action available once the player reaches a suitable location (usually Bridge or a still-functioning communications node). The roll determines how clean or contaminated the outgoing record is.

### Ontological Pressure
High Clubs and certain Diamonds can force direct confrontation with the fact that the ship’s interior no longer obeys consistent physical rules.

---

## Ship Layout (Static, Progressive Collapse)

```
Bridge
  |
Crew Quarters ─── Laboratory
  |               |
Engineering ─── Observation
  |
Airlock
```

**Starting Zone:** Crew Quarters  

**Critical Zones:** Bridge (transmission), Airlock (possible exit)

Zones may become rewritten or sealed as the transformation advances.

---

## Tracks

Standard starting values.  
**Mind** is under the heaviest pressure from geometry and ontological shifts.  
**Hope** erodes as the playable volume of the ship shrinks.  
**Integrity** suffers when the ship’s changes become physically dangerous.

---

## Suit Guidance (CS Orpheus)

| Suit       | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **Spades** | Physical danger from shifting structure, sudden seals, hostile geometry |
| **Hearts** | Crew traces, personal logs, emotional residue of those already incorporated |
| **Diamonds** | Ship systems, maps, records, the mechanics of the rewriting |
| **Clubs** | Direct cosmic / ontological pressure, the true nature of the transformation |

---

## Success Condition

Send a Final Transmission that survives the ship’s editing, or reach a still-viable exit, while retaining at least 1 Hope.  
Contaminated or costly success still counts if some record or the player themselves escapes the full transformation.

---

## Failure Conditions

- Any track reaches 0  
- The last human-compatible volume is rewritten while you are inside it  
- You are fully incorporated into the ship’s new state

---

## Opening Log Seed

> Woke to the sound of the hull rewriting itself.  
> Bulkheads that were straight yesterday now curve. The ship’s internal map no longer matches the corridors.  
> I am still alone. The log says the rest of the crew left. The airlocks say they never opened.

---

## Design Notes

- The ship itself is the antagonist and the environment.  
- Progressive loss of reliable space and records creates mounting pressure.  
- Success is usually partial or costly; clean escape from a transforming vessel is rare.  
- Serves as the foundational “pure” cosmic-rewriting module of the set.
