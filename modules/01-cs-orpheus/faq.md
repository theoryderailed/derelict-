# CS Orpheus — FAQ

**Q: Is the ship actively hostile?**  
A: It is not hostile in a conventional sense. It is transforming according to its own logic. Human-compatible spaces and records are side effects being edited away.

**Q: Can I stop the transformation?**  
A: Complete reversal is not the expected outcome. Success is usually measured by transmitting a record, escaping a final volume, or simply remaining coherent longer than the ship expects.

**Q: What does “the ship is rewriting itself” mean in play?**  
A: Corridors change, doors disappear or appear, maps become unreliable, and physical laws inside the hull begin to drift. Cards (especially Clubs and Diamonds) drive these changes.

**Q: Do I need to visit every zone?**  
A: No. The layout exists to give pressure and limited movement options. Many playthroughs stay in a shrinking set of still-human volumes.

**Q: What is the Final Transmission Roll?**  
A: When the player attempts to send a lasting record or reach a last viable exit, a final roll (modified by current tracks) determines how clean or contaminated the ending is. Even a costly transmission can count as success if the log survives.
