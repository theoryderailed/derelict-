# Gantry — FAQ

**Q: How does Standing change?**  
A: Raising Standing usually requires visible conformity (not looking into the dark, not asking about the Walkers, taking extra alignment shifts). Lowering Standing happens when you are seen staring into the dark, asking questions, or refusing work.

**Q: What is the “What You See in the Dark” table for?**  
A: When a card or situation calls for cosmic exposure, use the table (or roll d12) to generate a specific vision. Higher rank cards produce closer, more personal, or more prolonged visions.

**Q: Does looking at the cosmic always lower Standing?**  
A: Not automatically, but the crew notices when someone starts “seeing.” Being observed while having a vision is one of the main ways Standing drops.

**Q: Can I raise Standing after it falls?**  
A: Yes, through continued visible conformity and useful work, but recovery is slower once the crew has marked you as a problem.

**Q: What happens at Standing 0?**  
A: You become a liability. “Accidents” become likely and the crew may actively work against you. Escape becomes much harder.
