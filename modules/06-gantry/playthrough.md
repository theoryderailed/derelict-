# Gantry — Example Playthrough

**Setup**  
Tracks: Integrity 6 | Mind 6 | Hope 4  
Standing: 2  
Zone: Main Scaffold Hub

**Opening**  
Another exterior shift. Company channel still dead. Two more empty harnesses on the rack this morning. No one mentioned them. The deep spars are longer than last rotation. I measured. When I said so in the hab, conversation stopped. I go back out anyway.

**Cycle 1**  
Card 4♥  
Quiet meal. Subject changed when the Walkers came up. Standing stays 2.  
*Log:* Looking at the empty hooks is starting to feel rude.

**Cycle 2**  
Card 8♣ → Cost → –1 Mind  
Vision: Something enormous passes behind the farthest spars. It takes nearly a full minute. No one on the channel comments.  
*Log:* I saw it. Whatever is using us as a frame moved. I came back inside and said nothing. Mind 5.

**Cycle 3**  
Card 9♥  
Crew assigns extra “alignment” shift on the Growth Edge. Accept (conform). Standing → 3.  
Work is close to the dark. Another vision leaks through (impossible geometry).  
*Log:* I took the shift. They stopped watching me so hard. The spar ends in a place the lights won’t touch.

**Cycle 4**  
Card King of Clubs → Failure → –2 Mind  
Full vision: For a moment the Gantry is transparent. You understand what it is scaffolding. Standing drops to 1 (crew noticed).  
*Log:* I know what we’re building. I can’t write it down. The words don’t fit. Status 1. Mind 3.

**Cycle 5**  
Card 7♠ → Cost → –1 Integrity  
An “accident” on the scaffold. Safety line fails at the wrong moment. Barely catch yourself. No one nearby to help.  
*Log:* The line parted like it was cut. Standing at 1 means I’m already a problem they need to solve. Integrity 5.

Moved toward the Damaged Shuttle Dock while the crew is occupied.

**Cycle 6**  
Card Ace of Hearts  
A Walker’s voice comes over local comms — calm, familiar, inviting you to end of shift. Standing low enough that two crew members start moving in your direction.  
Reach the Shuttle Dock under pressure.

**Final Escape Roll:** 5  
Modifiers: –1 (Mind 3), +0 (Hope 4), –1 (low Standing / crew interference)  
Result: 3 → Contaminated success

**Final Log**  
Shuttle released on residual power. The crew didn’t make it to the airlock in time. I can still see the Gantry in the rear camera. It is longer than it was when I left. Something behind it is patient. I don’t know if I got away clean, or if I just carried the frame with me.

**Final State:** Integrity 5 | Mind 3 | Hope 4 | Contaminated escape
