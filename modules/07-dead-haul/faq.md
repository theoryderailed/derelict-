# Dead Haul — FAQ

**Q: When can I confront the killer?**  
A: Certainty 3 is the safe threshold. Certainty 2 allows a risky attempt (the killer has a strong chance to turn it on you). Below that is almost always lethal.

**Q: Do Spades or Hearts ever raise Certainty?**  
A: No. Only Diamonds and Clubs that represent real evidence or anomalies.

**Q: What does an Ace of Clubs or Diamonds give me?**  
A: +2 Certainty on a clean success. These are major breakthroughs.

**Q: What happens when the Victim Clock gets high?**  
A: The killer begins hunting you directly. New bodies appear faster. Social contact dries up completely.

**Q: Can I lower the Victim Clock?**  
A: No. It only advances. Your job is to reach usable Certainty before it maxes out.
