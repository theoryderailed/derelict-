# Quarantined — Example Playthrough

**Setup**  
Tracks: Integrity 6 | Mind 6 | Hope 4  
Infection Track: 0  
Research Progress: 0  
Patient Stability: 3  
Zone: Nursing Station

**Opening**  
Supervisor died on the second night. Ring sealed six hours later. Remote doctors on the line with a three-second lag. Twelve responsive patients and one who keeps saying the colony will eventually burn the ring clean. I don’t know if I’m saving anyone. I only know I haven’t stopped yet.

**Cycle 1**  
Card 6♦ → Clean  
First viable samples sent. Research Progress → 1  
*Log:* Confirmed receipt. Lag is bad but the channel is open.

**Cycle 2**  
Card 8♥  
The Doubter speaks at length. “Every hour you keep us alive is another hour they have to decide we’re not worth the risk.”  
–1 Mind for engaging.  
*Log:* I listened. I shouldn’t have. Mind 5.

**Cycle 3**  
Card 9♠ → Cost → –1 Integrity  
Delirious patient breached restraints. Contained, but exposure risk.  
Infection Track → 1  
*Log:* Blood on the gloves. Protocol held — mostly.

**Cycle 4**  
Card Ace of Diamonds → Clean  
Symptom progression does not match the remote model. Research Progress → 3  
*Log:* Sent the correction. They went quiet, then asked for more.

**Cycle 5**  
Card 7♣  
Anomalous symptom cluster in two patients. Patient Stability → 2. –1 Hope  
*Log:* It’s not behaving like a normal pathogen. Or I’m losing perspective.

**Cycle 6**  
Card 5♥  
The Doubter again, quieter. Limited engagement. No further Mind loss.  
*Log:* Checked vitals and left. No argument today.

**Cycle 7**  
Card 10♦ → Cost → –1 Integrity  
Supply shortage. Reused protective gear. Infection Track → 2  
Research Progress → 4  
*Log:* Progress 4. Infection 2. Remote team says they have a candidate protocol. Need three more cycles of stable data.

**Cycle 8–9**  
One acute crisis contained. One patient lost (Stability 1). External query arrives asking for “contingency readiness.”  
Hope 2. Mind 4. Infection still 2.

**Cycle 10**  
Remote doctors transmit workable treatment outline. First doses given under remaining protections.  
Final checks hold. Infection stays at 2.  
**Success.**

**Final Log**  
First patients responding. The Doubter went quiet when the fever broke. I still don’t know whether the colony would have burned us. I only know we made it long enough for the data to matter. Infection 2. I can live with that. I think.

**Final State:** Integrity 4 | Mind 4 | Hope 2 | Infection 2 | Research 4
