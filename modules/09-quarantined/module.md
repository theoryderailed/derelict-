# Module 09: Quarantined

**A Derelict module**  
Tone: Medical horror, moral uncertainty, isolation under pressure  
Length: Medium (9–14 cycles)  
Complexity: Medium

---

## Premise

You are a nurse on a habitat ring that has been sealed off from the rest of the colony. Your supervisor is already dead from a rapidly spreading illness. Remote doctors are working with you over a laggy connection, trying to understand the pathogen and develop a treatment. Your job is to keep the infected alive long enough for that work to succeed — without becoming one of them.

One of the patients is convinced there is no cure. They believe the larger colony will eventually decide the entire ring is a write-off and destroy it to protect everyone else. Every treatment you give, every sample you send, every hour you keep people alive may be buying time for a cure… or simply delaying the inevitable purge.

You never know which it is.

---

## Your Role

Nurse / sole remaining medical staff inside the sealed habitat ring. You have limited supplies, limited reliable information, and a direct line to doctors who cannot reach you physically.

---

## Running Theme: Help or Delay the End?

Success is measured by how much useful data and stabilized patients you can deliver before the situation collapses. Failure is personal infection or the ring being written off.

---

## Special Module Tools

### Infection Track (Personal, 0–4)
Rises when you treat patients under poor conditions, fail protective measures, or take certain risky actions.  
Reaching 4 = you are infected (hard fail or severely compromised ending).

### Research Progress (0–4)
Advanced by successful samples, observations, and treatments reported to the remote doctors.  
You need high Progress for the cure path to become available.

### Patient Stability
A simple collective measure of how the infected in your care are doing.  
Letting it collapse costs Hope and can accelerate external pressure.

### The Doubter
One recurring patient (or a shifting voice among them) who argues that continued treatment only prolongs suffering and makes the eventual destruction of the ring more certain.  
Engaging with them can cost Mind or Hope. Ignoring them entirely also carries consequences.

### External Pressure
The longer the quarantine lasts without clear progress, the more likely the larger colony is to consider terminal options. This functions as a soft escalating threat.

```
        |

```

**Starting Zone:** Nursing Station  

**Critical Zones:**  
- Ward A / Ward B — patients and the Doubter  
- Supply Closet / Sample Prep — research actions  
- Airlock / Communication Node — contact with remote doctors and the outside

---

## Tracks

Standard starting values.  
**Integrity** is pressured by exhaustion, injury, and failed protective measures.  
**Mind** erodes from the Doubter, isolation, and moral weight.  
**Hope** drops when patients deteriorate or External Pressure rises.

| Suit       | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **Spades** | Acute medical crisis, containment breaches, violence from delirious patients |
| **Hearts** | Patients, the Doubter, emotional cost, personal connections |
| **Diamonds** | Supplies, equipment, samples, remote doctor communications, research |
| **Clubs** | The illness itself, anomalous symptoms, the growing sense that something is wrong with the disease |

---

## Success Condition

Reach sufficient Research Progress (usually 3–4) and keep enough patients (and yourself) stable long enough for the remote doctors to confirm a viable treatment path. You must still be uninfected or only marginally exposed.

---

## Failure Conditions

- Personal Infection Track reaches 4  
- Patient Stability collapses completely  
- External authorities decide the ring is lost  
- Mind or Hope reaches 0

---

## Opening Log Seed

> Supervisor died on the second night. Ring sealed six hours later.  
> Remote doctors are on the line with a three-second lag. They want samples, observations, anything.  
> I have twelve patients who are still responsive and one who keeps telling me that none of this matters — that the colony is already calculating how much fuel it will take to burn the ring clean.  
> I don’t know if I’m saving anyone. I only know I haven’t stopped yet.

---

## Design Notes

- Medical and moral pressure are the twin engines.  
- The Doubter provides a persistent voice of fatalism that the player must decide how to engage with.  
- Research Progress vs Infection Track creates a clear risk/reward loop around treating patients.  
- External Pressure keeps the larger colony present as a distant, possibly terminal threat.  
- Fits Derelict’s isolation tone while adding a distinct caregiving and ethical dimension.
