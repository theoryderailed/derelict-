# Quarantined — FAQ

**Q: What raises the Infection Track?**  
A: Treating patients under poor conditions, failing protective measures, or taking risky actions (especially on Spades or when supplies are low). Reaching 4 means you are infected.

**Q: How do I raise Research Progress?**  
A: Successful samples, observations, and treatments reported to the remote doctors (mainly clean or partial successes on Diamonds and relevant Clubs).

**Q: Do I have to engage with the Doubter?**  
A: No, but ignoring them entirely also carries consequences. Limited or careful engagement is usually the middle path.

**Q: What happens if Patient Stability collapses?**  
A: Hope drops and External Pressure accelerates. Complete collapse can end the module.

**Q: Is the larger colony actually going to destroy the ring?**  
A: That remains uncertain by design. The module never confirms it one way or the other. The doubt is the point.
