# Static — Example Playthrough

**Setup**  
Tracks: Integrity 6 | Mind 6 | Hope 4  
Rescuer Track: 0  
Zone: Communications

**Opening**  
Distress beacon has been running for eleven days. Today something answered. A voice on the emergency channel — calm, professional, claiming to be a rescue cutter three hours out. It knows my name. It knows the ship’s registry. I do not remember sending either.

**Cycle 1**  
Card 5♦  
Minor power fluctuation in the antenna array. No roll.  
*Log:* Signal still clean. Voice still there. Rescuer Track 0.

**Cycle 2**  
Card 8♥ → Cost → –1 Mind  
The voice asks for a status report in the exact phrasing I used in an old log I never transmitted.  
*Log:* It should not know that sentence. Mind 5.

**Cycle 3**  
Card 9♣ → Clean  
Anomalous return on the long-range scan. Something is using the beacon as a beacon of its own. Rescuer Track → 1.  
*Log:* The signal is being repeated outward. Not just received. Track 1.

**Cycle 4**  
Card 7♠ → Failure → –1 Integrity, –1 Hope  
Antenna alignment slips. Voice becomes urgent, almost pleading. “Do not shut it down. We are close.”  
*Log:* It sounded afraid. Or it wanted me to think it was. Integrity 5, Hope 3.

**Cycle 5**  
Card Ace of Clubs → Cost → –1 Mind  
Clear evidence the original distress call is being used as a lure. Multiple ship signatures have approached this vector and gone silent. Rescuer Track → 3.  
*Log:* I am not the first. The voice is still talking. Mind 4. Track 3.

**Cycle 6**  
Decision point. Player chooses to shut down the antenna.  
Final confrontation with the signal / entity.  
Roll under pressure: 4 → Cost. –1 Mind. Antenna powered down.  
**Success (ambiguous).**

**Final Log**  
Beacon silent. The voice continued for another forty minutes after the carrier died, then stopped mid-sentence. No cutter arrived. No wreckage on the scopes. I am still here. The static is quieter now. I do not know if I saved myself or simply made the next ship someone else’s problem.

**Final State:** Integrity 5 | Mind 3 | Hope 3 | Antenna offline
