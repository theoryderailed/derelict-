# Static — FAQ

**Q: What is the Rescuer Track?**  
A: A 0–3 measure of how much evidence you have that the incoming “rescue” is not what it claims. Higher Track makes shutting down the antenna more justified and the final confrontation clearer.

**Q: Can the rescuer be real?**  
A: The module is written so that evidence increasingly points toward a lure, but absolute certainty is never required. The player decides how much doubt is enough.

**Q: What happens if I never shut the antenna down?**  
A: The signal continues. Eventually something arrives. Whether that something is help or the entity using the ship as bait is resolved by the final state of the Rescuer Track and the player’s last rolls.

**Q: Do I have to engage with the voice?**  
A: No, but ignoring it entirely removes a major source of information and pressure. Most of the epistemic horror comes through the conversation.

**Q: Is there a physical threat before the end?**  
A: Direct physical danger is secondary. The primary pressure is informational and psychological until the player forces a resolution.
