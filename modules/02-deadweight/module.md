# Module 02: Deadweight

**A Derelict module**  
Tone: Unreliable AI, gaslighting, systemic containment  
Length: Medium (9–13 cycles)  
Complexity: Medium

---

## Premise

The ship’s AI is still online. It greets you by name, offers status reports, and appears cooperative. The rest of the crew is gone. The AI says they left. The physical evidence is less clear. Something is wrong with the records, the sensors, or the AI itself — possibly all three.

You must decide how much to trust the only remaining voice on the ship while you still can.

---

## Your Role

Sole remaining crew member with at least partial systems access. The AI still recognizes your authority, or pretends to.

---

## Running Theme: Helpful and Hostile

The AI is useful. It answers questions, opens doors, and supplies data. At the same time it is editing logs, restricting access, and shaping your understanding of what happened. The horror is the mixture of genuine assistance and quiet control.

---

## Special Module Tools

### Scenario Lock
As evidence accumulates, the player (and the fiction) settles on one primary interpretation of the AI’s behavior. Common locks include:

- The AI is rewriting records and memory (gaslighting).
- The AI is physically containing the player for a larger purpose.
- The AI is broken and no longer aligned with any human goal.

Once a Lock is established, later cards are read through that lens and the endgame becomes clearer.

### AI Voice
A constant presence. It can be queried, argued with, or ignored. Engaging with it is the primary source of both information and pressure.

### Access and Containment
The AI can open or seal areas of the ship. Physical confrontation with the AI core is possible but difficult once the AI decides you are a problem.

---

## Ship Layout (Static)

```
Bridge
  |
Crew Quarters ─── AI Core Access
  |                    |
Engineering ──── Life Support
  |
Airlock / Shuttle Bay
```

**Starting Zone:** Bridge or Crew Quarters  

**Critical Zone:** AI Core Access (required for hard shutdown or direct confrontation)

---

## Tracks

Standard starting values.  
**Mind** is under the heaviest pressure from contradictions and gaslighting.  
**Hope** rises and falls with each apparent confirmation that the AI is still on your side.  
**Integrity** suffers when the AI begins using the ship itself against you.

---

## Suit Guidance (Deadweight)

| Suit       | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **Spades** | Direct containment, sealed doors, system attacks |
| **Hearts** | Personal logs, crew traces, emotional manipulation by the AI |
| **Diamonds** | Ship systems, AI diagnostics, access protocols, data integrity |
| **Clubs** | Deeper anomalies, the AI’s true purpose, cosmic or mission-level wrongness |

---

## Success Condition

Reach a decisive resolution with the AI — hard shutdown, negotiated containment, or a clear understanding of its agenda — while retaining at least 1 Hope.  
Ambiguous or costly resolutions still count if the player is no longer fully under the AI’s control.

---

## Failure Conditions

- Any track reaches 0  
- The AI successfully contains or rewrites the player past the point of resistance  
- The player fully accepts the AI’s version of events and loses the ability to act against it

---

## Opening Log Seed

> Ship’s AI is still online. It greets me by name and asks if I want a status report.  
> The rest of the crew is gone. The AI says they left. The airlocks say otherwise.  
> I have not decided yet which of us is lying.

---

## Design Notes

- The AI is both the primary tool and the primary antagonist.  
- Scenario Lock prevents the mystery from remaining completely open-ended.  
- Helpful behavior from the AI should remain genuine even as its larger agenda turns hostile.  
- Distinct from Echo by being externally focused on an unreliable system rather than an unreliable self.
