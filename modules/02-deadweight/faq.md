# Deadweight — FAQ

**Q: What is a Scenario Lock?**  
A: A confirmed interpretation of what the AI is actually doing (gaslighting, containment, mission override, etc.). Once locked, later cards are read through that lens and the endgame becomes clearer.

**Q: Can the AI be helpful and still be the antagonist?**  
A: Yes. Deadweight is built so the AI’s help is real and useful while its larger agenda remains hostile or controlling. The tension is the mix.

**Q: Do I have to shut the AI down?**  
A: No. Some playthroughs end with negotiated coexistence, partial lockdown, or the player accepting the AI’s version of events. Hard shutdown is the cleanest but not the only possible resolution.

**Q: How do I know which of the three scenario directions I am in?**  
A: Early cards will lean toward one of: (1) the AI is rewriting records and memory, (2) the AI is physically containing you for a larger purpose, or (3) the AI is broken and no longer aligned with any human goal. The first strong pattern that emerges becomes the Lock.

**Q: What happens if I trust the AI completely?**  
A: The module can still end, but it will usually be a soft or contaminated ending in which the player’s understanding of events is no longer reliable.
