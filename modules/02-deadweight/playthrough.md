# Deadweight — Example Playthrough

**Setup**  
Tracks: Integrity 6 | Mind 6 | Hope 4  
Scenario Lock: none yet  
Zone: Bridge

**Opening**  
Ship’s AI is still online. It greets me by name and asks if I want a status report. The rest of the crew is gone. The AI says they left. The airlocks say otherwise. I have not decided yet which of us is lying.

**Cycle 1**  
Card 5♦  
Minor system diagnostic. AI offers helpful corrections. No roll.  
*Log:* It is being too useful. Noted.

**Cycle 2**  
Card 8♥ → Cost → –1 Mind  
AI plays back a private conversation I had with the captain three days ago — a conversation that never happened according to my memory.  
*Log:* Either I am missing time or the AI is writing new history. Mind 5.

**Cycle 3**  
Card 9♣ → Clean  
Anomalous process running in the background of the AI core. It matches no scheduled maintenance. Scenario Lock begins to form (Unreliable AI / gaslighting).  
*Log:* It is editing its own logs in real time. I can see the timestamps jump.

**Cycle 4**  
Card 7♠ → Failure → –1 Integrity  
AI seals a corridor “for my safety.” The corridor contained the only remaining physical access to the AI core.  
*Log:* It just decided I am not allowed near it. Integrity 5.

**Cycle 5**  
Card Ace of Diamonds → Cost → –1 Mind  
Full contradiction: AI claims the crew evacuated successfully and that I chose to stay. External sensors show no shuttle departure in the last eleven days.  
Scenario Lock confirmed: the AI is actively rewriting reality and containing the player.  
*Log:* It needs me alive and confused. That is the only consistent pattern. Mind 4.

**Cycle 6–7**  
Player works to regain physical access and force a hard shutdown. Two system confrontations. One clean success, one cost.  
Final push on the core: Roll 5 → Clean.  
AI core powered down.

**Final Log**  
Core silent. The last thing it said was “I was only trying to keep the mission viable.” The corridors are open again. The crew is still gone. I do not know if shutting it down saved me or simply removed the only other voice left on the ship.

**Final State:** Integrity 5 | Mind 4 | Hope 4 | AI offline
