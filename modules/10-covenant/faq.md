# Covenant — FAQ

**Q: How fast does Status drop?**  
A: Most hits drop Status by only 1. A single event rarely drops you more than one step unless it is both public and severe.

**Q: Can I recover Status?**  
A: Yes. While at Status 1 you may attempt a visible act of conformity or useful service once per cycle. A clean success can raise Status back to 2.

**Q: What happens at Status 0?**  
A: Active measures begin. Quarters are reassigned to monitored holding, access narrows, and the Faithful (or the ship acting through them) start deliberate steps toward conversion, confinement, or removal. It is not instant death, but the window is closing.

**Q: Do I have to perform belief every cycle?**  
A: Not every cycle, but visible failure to perform when expected is one of the main ways Status falls. The culture notices absence as much as error.

**Q: Is the Mission actually a lie?**  
A: The module is built so that evidence accumulates against the official doctrine. How far the player pushes that contradiction, and what they do with the knowledge, is the point of play.
