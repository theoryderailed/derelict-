# Module 10: Covenant

**A Derelict module**  
Tone: Generational cosmic horror, living religion, social exclusion  
Length: Medium to long (10–15 cycles)  
Complexity: Medium

---

## Premise

You live on a generation ship that has been traveling for centuries. The official doctrine is that the Mission is sacred and still on course. The culture that grew around that doctrine is rigid, watchful, and devout. Public recitations, shift rituals, and quiet surveillance are part of daily life. Questioning the Mission is heresy. Looking too closely at the older logs or the sealed forward sections is dangerous.

You have already been marked. Your questions, your time in restricted records, or simply the way you failed to perform belief correctly has placed you outside the Faithful. Conversations stop when you approach. Work assignments become worse. The formal and informal systems of the ship treat you as a problem to be corrected or removed.

You are surrounded by people and completely alone. The ship feels alive, crowded, and hostile in equal measure. Something is wrong with the voyage itself. The deeper records and the behavior of the oldest systems suggest the ship is not going where the doctrine claims — or that arrival was never the point.

---

## Your Role

Low-level systems tech, historian, or junior officer who has already fallen out of favor. You still have limited access and a place to sleep, but your standing is gone.

---

## Running Theme: Perform or Be Removed

The daily pressure is social and religious. You must decide how much belief to fake, how much truth to pursue, and how long you can remain useful enough not to be quietly erased. The cosmic truth of the Mission is the deeper horror; the living culture is the immediate one.

---

## Special Module Tools

### Standing / Faith Status (0–3)
Measures how tolerated you still are by the Faithful and the ship’s informal hierarchy.

| Status | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| 3      | Provisionally accepted. Limited friction. |
| 2      | Watched. Conversations thin. Assignments worsen. |
| 1      | Openly suspect. Access restricted. Soft sabotage begins. Recovery is still possible. |
| 0      | Outcast. Active measures begin (see below). The window is closing. |

**Status rules:**
- Most failures or discovered contradictions drop Status by only 1.
- While at Status 1 you may attempt a visible act of conformity or useful service once per cycle (correct recitation under scrutiny, high-risk work taken without complaint, etc.). A clean success can raise Status back to 2.
- Status 0 is a threshold, not instant removal.

### Active Measures (Status 0)
When Status reaches 0 the ship and the Faithful begin deliberate steps to resolve you:

- **Immediate:** Quarters reassigned to a monitored or temporary holding section. Communal movement draws open scrutiny. Some access revoked.
- **Short-term (1–2 cycles):** Punitive or isolating work. Formal “reflection” or doctrinal counseling required. Soft sabotage increases.
- **Active resolution:** Public reckoning, one-way assignment toward the Forward sections, or a medical/spiritual intervention. The character may still resist or attempt escape, but the margin is small.

### Doctrine vs Evidence
Major contradictions between official doctrine and recovered records raise Mind pressure and can force Status loss if the contradiction becomes known.

### The Faithful
The living population. The culture rewards public piety and punishes visible doubt. Individual cracks exist, but they are dangerous for everyone involved.

### Restricted Decks / Older Systems
Primary source of cosmic horror and a reliable way to destroy remaining Status.

## Ship Layout (Static – Lived-In)

```
Communal Hab / Recitation Hall
        |
Work Decks ─── Your Quarters
        |
Archive / Restricted Access
        |
Forward Sealed Sections
```

**Starting Zone:** Your Quarters or Work Decks  

**Critical Zones:**  
- Communal Hab / Recitation Hall — social pressure and performance of belief  
- Archive / Restricted Access — evidence and contradiction  
- Forward Sealed Sections — the deepest cosmic layer  

The ship should feel populated. Movement through communal areas carries social risk.

---

## Tracks

Standard starting values.  
**Mind** is pressured by contradiction and the strain of performance.  
**Hope** erodes as Status falls and the Mission’s true nature becomes clearer.  
**Integrity** suffers from poor assignments, “accidents,” and eventual active measures.

---

## Suit Guidance (Covenant)

| Suit       | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **Spades** | Direct social or physical threat, “accidents,” enforcement |
| **Hearts** | The Faithful, personal relationships, the cost of performance or honesty |
| **Diamonds** | Ship systems, records, archives, evidence of the real Mission |
| **Clubs** | Cosmic truth, the sealed sections, the sense that the voyage itself is wrong |

---

## Success Condition

Reach a decisive understanding of the Mission’s true nature and either:
- Escape the ship’s control (rare and difficult),  
- Carve out a tenable position that lets you continue existing without full submission or erasure, or  
- Force a rupture in the doctrine that cannot be quietly contained.

Survival with knowledge is the minimum bar.

---

## Failure Conditions

- Status reaches 0 and the ship successfully completes active measures against you  
- Mind or Hope reaches 0  
- You fully submit and become another voice of the doctrine (soft failure)

## Opening Log Seed

> They stopped sitting with me at recitation three cycles ago.  
> Work assignments have been the worst sections for a week. No one says why. They don’t have to.  
> I still know the words. I can still say them in the right order with the right expression.  
> The older logs do not match the words.  
> I have not decided yet whether that matters more than being allowed to keep existing here.

---

## Design Notes

- Social exclusion is the primary isolation engine. The ship is crowded and alive.  
- Religious performance is a daily mechanical and narrative pressure.  
- Status is a contested resource: it drops in steps and can be partially recovered from 1 back to 2.  
- Status 0 begins a clear escalation ladder rather than instant removal.  
- Cosmic horror is layered underneath and revealed through restricted access and contradiction.  
- Distinct from Gantry by being explicitly doctrinal and generational.  
- Distinct from the more solitary modules by being densely populated and socially mediated.
