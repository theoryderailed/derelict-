# Covenant — Example Playthrough

**Setup**  
Tracks: Integrity 6 | Mind 6 | Hope 4  
Status: 2  
Zone: Your Quarters

**Opening**  
They stopped sitting with me at recitation three cycles ago. Work assignments have been the worst sections for a week. I still know the words. The older logs do not match the words.

**Cycle 1**  
Card 5♥  
Corridor silence. No direct hit. Status stays 2.  
*Log:* The quiet is consistent. I am still being measured.

**Cycle 2**  
Card 8♦ → Clean  
Secondary archive access. Old maintenance log shows destination coordinates that no longer exist in official charts.  
*Log:* The numbers were real once. Now they are not spoken.

**Cycle 3**  
Card 9♥  
Public recitation under scrutiny. Performance correct. –1 Mind. Status stays 2.  
*Log:* Every gesture was right. The mask held. Mind 5.

**Cycle 4**  
Card 7♠ → Cost → –1 Integrity  
Work-deck “accident.” Status drops to 1.  
*Log:* They called it equipment failure. Everyone heard the real meaning. Status 1.

**Cycle 5**  
Card 6♥  
Former partner risks a short private word: “Some of us still read the old numbers.” +1 Hope.  
*Log:* Cracks exist. Small ones.

**Cycle 6 — Recovery Attempt**  
Volunteer for high-risk visible repair + correct recitation afterward.  
Roll 5 → Clean. Status recovers to 2.  
*Log:* I gave them performance and usefulness. They accepted it. For now.

**Cycle 7**  
Card Ace of Clubs → Cost → –1 Mind  
Deep diagnostic. Long-term trajectory has been adjusting for decades under “doctrinal correction.”  
*Log:* We are being steered. Not toward any arrival I was taught.

**Cycle 8**  
Card King of Diamonds → Clean  
Deeper archive. Original charter language around “arrival” is theological, not navigational.  
*Log:* We were never primarily a transport. We were the offering.

**Cycle 9**  
Card 9♣  
Forward Sealed Sections show low-level activation. Faithful call it blessing. Systems call it threshold. –1 Mind.  
*Log:* Something beyond the seal is answering the doctrine. Mind 3.

**Cycle 10**  
Public scrutiny returns. Private accounting required. Careful partial truth instead of full performance.  
Roll 3 → Cost. Status drops to 1. –1 Hope.  
*Log:* I did not fully kneel. They noticed.

**Cycle 11**  
At Status 1 try visible service again. Roll 4 → Cost. Recovery fails. –1 Integrity.  
*Log:* The performance was not enough this time.

**Cycle 12**  
Card 8♠ → Failure  
Second deliberate incident while isolated. Status drops to 0. Active measures begin.  
*Log:* Status 0. Quarters changed. Movement watched. The window is now measured in cycles.

**Current State:** Integrity 4 | Mind 3 | Hope 4 | Status 0  
Active measures underway. Agency remains, but the ship has begun resolving the problem.
