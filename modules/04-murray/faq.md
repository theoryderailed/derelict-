# Murray — FAQ

**Q: What exactly causes a loop reset?**  
A: Any result that is not a Clean Success (5–6) on a required roll ends the current loop immediately. You wake again in Quarters to the knocking.

**Q: Does Mind ever reset?**  
A: No. Mind is the only persistent track. Integrity and Hope reset to their starting values at the beginning of every new loop.

**Q: How does Mastery work?**  
A: The first time you achieve a Clean Success on a specific action or zone transition, note it. On all future loops that same action is free (no roll required).

**Q: Can I move freely between zones?**  
A: Only along the connections shown in the layout. Most pressure comes from the Clean Success requirement rather than zone status.

**Q: What if Mind reaches 0?**  
A: The character’s ability to continue coherently collapses. This is a hard failure state even if you have mastered the full route.
