# Module 04: Murray

**A Derelict module**  
Tone: Cold procedural horror, time-loop tension, emotionless alien  
Length: Short-to-medium (depends on learning speed)  
Complexity: Medium (strict success requirements + persistent Mind)

---

## Premise

You are a junior science assistant at a secure research facility. Against protocol, you have been privately studying a newly recovered lifeform. The entity is intelligent, emotionless, and has requested your help to leave.

The facility named it **Murray**.

Every time you fail, you wake again in your quarters to the sound of security knocking at the door. You remember everything. Murray is the cause of the loop.

---

## Your Role

Science assistant with limited access. You are not security, not senior research staff, and not supposed to be doing this.

---

## Running Theme: Cold Repetition

The horror is procedural and accumulating. Each failed attempt teaches you the facility a little better while eroding your Mind. Murray does not care how many times you wake up. It only wants to leave.

---

## Core Module Rules

### The Loop
- You always begin in your **Quarters** at the moment security begins knocking.
- You retain full memory across every loop.
- Murray is the source of the temporal reset. It may or may not acknowledge the repetition.

### Success & Failure
- Only a **Clean Success (5–6)** allows progress.
- Any **Success with Cost (3–4)** or **Failure (1–2)** immediately ends the current loop and triggers a reset.
- When the loop resets, you wake in your Quarters to the knocking.

### Tracks
- **Mind** is the only persistent track. It never resets between loops.
- **Integrity** and **Hope** reset to their starting values (6 and 4) at the beginning of every new loop.
- Each failed loop reduces Mind by 1.
- If Mind reaches 0, the character’s ability to continue coherently collapses.

### Mastery (Simple Version)
- The first time you achieve a Clean Success on a specific action or zone transition, note it.
- On all future loops, that same action is **free** (no roll required).
- Only actions you have personally cleaned become free.

### The Alien — Murray
- Emotionless. It wants to escape. It uses you because you are available and useful.
- It does not offer comfort, gratitude, or encouragement.
- It may provide precise information if doing so serves the escape.

### The Knocking
- Default: Facility security performing a check.
- May evolve in later loops if the fiction supports it.

## Facility Layout (Static)

```
Quarters
    |
Corridor Hub ─── Security Checkpoint
    |                    |
Research Labs ──── Containment
    |                    |
Service Tunnels ─── Loading Dock / Exit
```

**Starting Zone (every loop):** Quarters

**Critical Path:** Quarters → free Murray from Containment → reach the Loading Dock / Exit without being stopped.

**Connections (two-way):**
- Quarters ↔ Corridor Hub
- Corridor Hub ↔ Security Checkpoint
- Corridor Hub ↔ Research Labs
- Research Labs ↔ Containment
- Containment ↔ Service Tunnels
- Service Tunnels ↔ Loading Dock / Exit
- Security Checkpoint ↔ Service Tunnels (secondary, riskier)

Use standard Stable / Hazardous / Compromised rules if needed, but most pressure comes from the strict Clean Success requirement rather than zone status.

## Success Condition

Complete a single uninterrupted run from **Quarters → Containment → Loading Dock / Exit** while scoring only Clean Successes on every required roll (or using previously mastered free actions). Extract Murray without ever hitting a 1–4 on a new action.

---

## Failure Conditions

- Mind reaches 0
- You choose to stop attempting the escape
- The fiction supports a permanent collapse of the loop (rare)

---

## Opening Log Seed (Loop 1)

> Knocking at the door. Three sharp raps, pause, two more.  
> Security doing the morning check.  
> Murray is still in Containment. It is waiting.  
> It does not care how many times we have done this.

---

## Design Notes

- Extremely high tension on individual rolls.
- Knowledge and mastery are the primary forms of progress.
- Mind erosion creates a soft overall failure state even if the player keeps trying.
- The emotionless alien keeps the tone cold and procedural rather than sentimental.
- The simple mastery rule rewards careful learning without complex tracking.
- Core Derelict resolution and preferred tracks still apply; the module only changes the consequences of non-clean results and track persistence.
