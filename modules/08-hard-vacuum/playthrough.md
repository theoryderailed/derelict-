# Hard Vacuum — Example Playthrough

**Opening**  
Came to on the deck. Head ringing. Ribs wrong. Alarms everywhere. Most of the crew already dead. Found one still breathing near Medical. We got a partial signal out — rescue inbound. Then they died. I am alone. Hull Integrity already at 1. Rescue Timer set to 8.

**Cycle 1**  
Card 7♦ → Clean success.  
Patched a spreading micro-fracture in the Habitat Ring. Used one kit.  
*Log:* One less hiss. Timer 7.

**Cycle 2**  
Card 9♠ → Cost. –1 Integrity.  
Sudden pressure drop toward Engineering. Barely sealed it.  
*Log:* Almost lost the corridor. Two kits left. Timer 6.

**Cycle 3**  
Card 5♥  
Found the other survivor’s final note. –1 Hope.  
*Log:* They knew they weren’t making it. Told me to keep the Habitat sealed no matter what. Timer 5.

**Cycle 4**  
Card Ace of Diamonds → Failure. Hull Integrity advances to 2.  
Engineering cascades. Power to half the station drops. Sealed it off permanently.  
*Log:* Wrote Engineering off. Timer 4.

**Cycle 5–6**  
Two more structural hits. One successful emergency patch (last kit), one forced abandonment of Medical. Hull Integrity 3. Hope 2. Timer 2.  
*Log:* Habitat Ring and a sliver of Command are all that still hold. Suit almost dry.

**Cycle 7**  
Card 6♠ → Cost. –1 Integrity.  
Final micro-fracture in the Habitat itself. Threw everything left at it. It held.  
*Log:* Hiss stopped. Timer 1.

**Cycle 8 — Rescue Arrives**  
Timer reaches 0. Still alive. Habitat still pressurized.  
**Success.**

**Final Log**  
Docking clamps. Voices on the emergency channel. I am sitting against the bulkhead with a dead suit and three sealed doors between me and the parts of the station I gave up. I don’t remember how the cascade started. I only remember keeping this one volume alive long enough.
