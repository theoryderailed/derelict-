# Module 08: Hard Vacuum

**A Derelict module**  
Tone: Cold environmental horror, progressive system failure, cosmic indifference  
Length: Medium (8–13 cycles)  
Complexity: Low–Medium

---

## Premise

You come to in pain and confusion. You were knocked unconscious. Short-term memory is patchy — you know you were injured, but the moments leading up to it are gone. Alarms are screaming. The station has suffered catastrophic failure. Most of the crew are already dead.

You find one other survivor, badly hurt. Together you manage a partial signal: a rescue craft is inbound. Shortly afterward that last survivor dies from their injuries.

You are alone with a dying station. You do not know what caused the cascade. You only know the seals are failing and help is theoretically coming if you can keep a pocket of air intact long enough.

The vacuum is patient.

---

## Your Role

Damage-control / senior technician. You still have some access and the remaining patch materials. No one else is left to use them.

---

## Running Theme: Hold the Line

Success is not escape under your own power. Success is still being alive and pressurized when the rescue craft arrives. Every patch buys cycles. Every failure permanently shrinks the livable volume.

---

## Special Module Tools

### Rescue Timer
A simple countdown (recommended 8–10 ticks).  
Starts once the inbound rescue is confirmed.  
Advances automatically each cycle (or every other cycle if you prefer slower pressure).  
When it reaches zero, the rescue arrives — if you are still alive and at least one viable volume remains.

### Hull Integrity (0–5)
Escalating structural failure track.  
Advances on certain Diamonds/Spades or when the fiction demands it.  
Each advance spreads fractures, kills seals, or cascades system damage.  
High values make zones permanently Hazardous or Compromised and increase Exposure risk.

### Exposure
Moving through damaged or unpowered sections risks vacuum or radiation effects. These hit Integrity and Mind hard. Temporary patches and suit time can mitigate but never eliminate the danger.

### The Outside (Late, Optional)
High Clubs may introduce the quiet sense that the void is attentive. This remains atmospheric and secondary. The primary enemy is physics.

### Scarcity
Patch kits, air, power cells, and suit charges are finite. Track them in the journal.

---

## Station Layout (Static, Progressive Collapse)

```
Command / Ops
      |
Habitat Ring ─── Engineering
      |               |
Medical Bay ─── Storage / Airlocks
      |
Outer Hull Access / EVA Staging
```

**Starting Zone:** Habitat Ring or near Medical (where you regain consciousness)

**Critical Zones:**  
- Engineering — power and life support  
- Habitat Ring — primary livable volume  
- Outer Hull Access — required for major external patches, extremely dangerous  
- Storage / Airlocks — last supplies

As Hull Integrity worsens, sections can be permanently lost.

---

## Tracks

Standard starting values.  
**Integrity** is under constant environmental attack.  
**Mind** erodes from isolation, alarms, memory gaps, and the knowledge that you are alone.  
**Hope** drops every time a major system or section is lost forever.

---

## Suit Guidance (Hard Vacuum)

| Suit       | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **Spades** | Acute physical danger — sudden decompression risk, debris, suit failure, injury |
| **Hearts** | Human traces — empty quarters, final logs, the dead survivor, personal cost |
| **Diamonds** | Systems and structure — power, seals, life support, patch success or failure |
| **Clubs** | The Outside — quiet wrongness in the vacuum, the sense of being observed through the hull |

---

## Success Condition

Keep at least one critical volume pressurized and yourself alive until the **Rescue Timer** reaches zero.  
You must have at least 1 Hope remaining when the rescue arrives.

---

## Failure Conditions

- Any track reaches 0  
- Hull Integrity reaches maximum and the last livable volume is lost before rescue  
- You are caught in rapid decompression with no protection

---

## Opening Log Seed

> Came to on the deck. Head ringing. Ribs wrong.  
> Alarms everywhere. I don’t remember the impact — just the pain and then nothing.  
> Most of the crew are already dead. Found one still breathing near Medical. Bad shape.  
> We got a partial signal through: rescue craft inbound. ETA unknown but real.  
> Then the bleeding wouldn’t stop. They’re gone.  
> I don’t know what happened to the station. I only know the seals are failing and I’m the only one left to hold them.

---

## Design Notes

- Extremely pure Derelict experience — the environment is the antagonist.  
- Rescue Timer vs Hull Integrity creates a clear race.  
- Memory loss at the start keeps the cause of the disaster ambiguous.  
- The death of the last other survivor locks in total isolation early.  
- “Something outside” is optional flavor; the module functions without it.
