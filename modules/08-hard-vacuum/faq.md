# Hard Vacuum — FAQ

**Q: What happens if Hull Integrity maxes out before the Rescue Timer?**  
A: The last livable volume is lost. You fail.

**Q: Can I repair Hull Integrity?**  
A: You can slow or temporarily stop advances with successful patches, but you cannot permanently reduce the track. Damage accumulates.

**Q: Do I have to move to patch things?**  
A: Only if the fiction places the problem in another zone. Many patches can be done from your current location if the systems connect.

**Q: What if I run out of patch kits?**  
A: Further structural problems become much harder or impossible to stop. You may have to abandon sections proactively.

**Q: Is “something outside” required?**  
A: No. It is optional atmospheric flavor. The module works as pure environmental survival.
