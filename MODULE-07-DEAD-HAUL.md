# Module 07: Dead Haul

**A Derelict module**  
Tone: Industrial pulp noir, claustrophobic survival horror, escalating dread  
Length: Medium (9–14 cycles)  
Complexity: Medium

---

## Premise

You are the sole remaining investigator on a mid-sized asteroid mining colony. A serial killer is active. The company wants the matter handled quietly before the next haul ship arrives. Most of the rock is under informal lockdown. People have stopped talking.

The first bodies looked like the usual — shift disputes, debts, jealousy. The later ones do not. Evidence is scarce, contradictory, or actively disappearing. You work mostly alone. The corridors between the occupied habs are long and poorly lit.

Whether the killer is human or something that only wears a human shape is something you will have to discover.

---

## Your Role

Colony security / detective. You have limited formal authority. The company expects results without attention. The miners expect you to stop the killings before they decide to handle it themselves.

---

## Running Theme: The Rock Is Small and Getting Smaller

Every new victim shrinks the circle of people still willing to speak to you. The killer is almost certainly someone you have already seen. Time is measured in bodies.

---

## Special Module Tools

### Victim Clock
A simple escalating pressure track.  
Advances on certain Spades/Hearts or when the fiction demands a new body.  
Each advance = a new victim (or a near-miss that still costs Hope/Mind).  
At high Clock the killer begins hunting you directly.

### Certainty (0–3)
Lightweight measure of how solid your case is.

**Rank-Sensitive Gains** (only from ♦ Diamonds and ♣ Clubs that represent real evidence or anomalies):

| Rank of card          | Clean Success (5–6) | Success with Cost (3–4) |
|-----------------------|---------------------|-------------------------|
| 2–5                   | +0                  | +0                      |
| 6–10                  | +1                  | +0 or +1                |
| Jack / Queen / King   | +1                  | +1                      |
| Ace                   | +2                  | +1                      |

- Failed rolls (1–2) never raise Certainty and may lower it if evidence is lost.
- Spades and Hearts never raise Certainty.

You need Certainty 3 for a relatively safe confrontation or proper trap.  
Certainty 2 allows a risky attempt (the killer has a strong chance to turn it on you).  
Acting at Certainty 0–1 is almost always lethal.

### The Pattern
The murders follow a rough rhythm and preferred locations/shift times. Learning the pattern is both investigative progress and survival information.

### Lonely Contact
Most residents are unavailable, hostile, or later become victims. Real conversations are rare, brief, and usually happen in exposed or unsafe places. The colony feels inhabited but you remain functionally isolated with the case.

---

## Colony Layout (Static, Compact)

```
Security Office / Your Desk
        |
Central Hab Ring ─── Medical / Morgue
        |
Mining Ops ─── Maintenance Tunnels
        |
Lower Docks / Storage
```

**Starting Zone:** Security Office  

**Critical Zones:**  
- Medical / Morgue — bodies and forensic traces  
- Maintenance Tunnels — preferred killing ground / movement routes  
- Lower Docks — possible escape or final confrontation site  
- Central Hab Ring — scarce social contact and rising tension

---

## Tracks

Standard starting values.  
**Hope** drops with every new victim.  
**Mind** drops from contradictions, isolation, and the growing sense that the case is wrong.  
**Integrity** suffers from chases, traps, and direct confrontation.

---

## Suit Guidance (Dead Haul)

| Suit       | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **♠ Spades** | Direct threat, the killer’s actions, violence, pursuit |
| **♥ Hearts** | Victims, witnesses, personal cost, the human toll |
| **♦ Diamonds** | Evidence, colony systems, company interference, physical clues |
| **♣ Clubs** | Anomalous traces, pattern breaks, the sense that something is wrong with the case itself |

---

## Success Condition

Reach Certainty 3 (or attempt at 2 under heavy risk) and confront or trap the killer before the Victim Clock maxes out or the killer reaches you.  

Stopping the killer (by exposure, capture, or lethal force) with at least 1 Hope remaining counts as success. The final confrontation is always dangerous.

---

## Failure Conditions

- The killer kills you (hard fail)  
- Victim Clock reaches maximum and the colony collapses into uncontrolled violence  
- Mind or Hope reaches 0 (you break or give up the case)

---

## Opening Log Seed

> Third body this rotation. Same shift window. Same quiet corridor between the lower habs and the processing plant.  
> Company wants it kept off the official channel. Next haul ship is still seventeen days out.  
> I have a desk, a sidearm, and a list of people who no longer answer when I knock.  
> The rock is small. Whoever is doing this is still on it.

---

## Design Notes

- Starts as pulp-noir investigation, slides into survival horror.  
- The killer’s true nature (human or anomalous) is revealed through play.  
- Rank-sensitive Certainty keeps progress tied to the cards while preventing permanent stalls.  
- Victim Clock creates intense time pressure and emotional weight.  
- Minimal contact keeps the Derelict isolation tone intact despite the colony setting.
