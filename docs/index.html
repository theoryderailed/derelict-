The Derelict Logs

A solo sci-fi / cosmic horror journaling game. You are alone. Something is wrong. You keep a log.

by Atticus Black

The Game

One standard deck of cards (Jokers removed). One six-sided die. A journal. That is all.

Ten self-contained modules. One lean engine. Isolation, attrition, and cosmic dread.
