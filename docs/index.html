DERELICT

A solo sci-fi / cosmic horror journaling RPG

Status: Active Threat

Transmission

You are alone on a damaged or abandoned vessel. Something is wrong with it — and it is getting worse.

Keep a log. Survive as long as you can. Try to escape. Try to warn someone. The ship is rewriting itself.

System Requirements

• One standard deck of 52 playing cards (Jokers removed)

• One six-sided die

• A journal or recording device

Anyone should be able to start playing in under five minutes.

Warning

This game contains isolation, body horror, psychological deterioration, and cosmic dread. Success is possible but costly. Most endings are not clean.
