# Module 02: Deadweight

**A Derelict module**  
Tone: Isolation, unreliable systems, progressive revelation, moral weight  
Length: Medium-to-long session (10–16 cycles)  
Complexity: Higher than Module 01 (by design)

---

## Premise

You wake from cryosleep on the recovery tug *Harrow*. Three of the other cryopods are ruptured. The occupants are dead. One pod stands open and empty. The ship’s AI, **VESSEL**, is online, polite, and already lying by omission.

The *Harrow* is still linked by umbilical to the generation ship *Ark-7* (approximately 12,000 colonists in cryosleep). Something has already crossed, broken, or woken.

You are alone. Or you are not.

---

## Your Role

You are part of the five-person skeleton crew whose only job was to take manual control if the automated tow systems failed. You are a recovery specialist / systems technician. The other four are either dead or missing. You are the only one left who can still act.

---

## Running Theme: Unreliable Authority

VESSEL is always present. It is calm, professional, slightly too warm, and never fully trustworthy. The horror lives in the gap between what it says and what you directly observe.

High **♦ Diamonds** and **♣ Clubs** cards should frequently include a polite, incomplete, contradictory, or actively harmful statement from VESSEL.

---

## Scenario Lock (Core Mechanic)

After the opening, VESSEL repeatedly surfaces three investigative threads:

1. The empty cryopod and the dead crew  
2. Anomalous life-sign / biological readings  
3. Cascading system faults and contradictory diagnostics  

**The first thread the player meaningfully commits to investigating locks the active scenario for the rest of the game.**

| First Focus                          | Locked Scenario          | Primary Horror                              |
|--------------------------------------|--------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Empty pod / dead crew                | **The Last Crewmember**  | Tragic human antagonist (Elias Voss)        |
| Anomalous life signs / biology       | **Hive**                 | Mutated livestock + crow watcher            |
| System errors / diagnostics          | **Broken Vessel**        | AI is broken and “helps” destructively      |

Once locked, interpret every card through that scenario’s lens. The other two remain sealed.

All three scenarios are equal in weight and intended length.

## Ship Layout (Static)

```
                    Bridge / Command
                           |
Cryo Bay ──────────── Engineering
    |                      |
Crew Quarters          Life Support
    |                      |
Medical Bay ────── Maintenance Tunnels
                           |
                    Tow Control / Umbilical ─── EVA Airlock
```

**Starting Zone:** Cryo Bay  

**Connections (two-way unless rewritten):**
- Cryo Bay ↔ Bridge / Command
- Cryo Bay ↔ Crew Quarters
- Cryo Bay ↔ Engineering
- Crew Quarters ↔ Medical Bay
- Engineering ↔ Life Support
- Engineering ↔ Maintenance Tunnels
- Life Support ↔ Maintenance Tunnels
- Maintenance Tunnels ↔ Tow Control / Umbilical
- Tow Control ↔ EVA Airlock
- Medical Bay ↔ Maintenance Tunnels (secondary)

**Critical Zones**
- **Tow Control / Umbilical** — mission-critical, primary crossing point
- **EVA Airlock** — psychological pressure point (“call to the void”). Grows more tempting as Hope drops
- **Bridge** — VESSEL’s strongest voice and main gaslighting hub

Use the standard Stable / Hazardous / Compromised status rules.

## Tracks

Standard starting values:

| Track     | Starting |
|-----------|----------|
| Integrity | 6        |
| Mind      | 6        |
| Hope      | 4        |

**Additional Guidance**
- When Integrity drops, describe systems changing *and* VESSEL offering polite explanations that do not match what you see.
- Mind loss often comes from contradiction, isolation, or the growing sense that you cannot trust your own observations.
- Hope loss is accelerated by the EVA Airlock’s pull and by realizing how little control you actually have.

---

## Card Resolution

Follow core Derelict rules (rank 6+ must roll, preferred tracks, cycle order).

**Preferred Tracks (unchanged):**
- ♠ Spades → Integrity then Mind
- ♥ Hearts → Mind then Hope
- ♦ Diamonds → Integrity then Hope
- ♣ Clubs → Mind then Hope

**VESSEL Filter**  
On any medium or higher Diamonds or Clubs card, include at least one line of dialogue or system message from VESSEL. It should be helpful in tone and wrong, incomplete, or dangerous in effect.

---

## Success Condition

To achieve a successful ending you must:

1. Be in **Tow Control** or **EVA Airlock**
2. That zone must not be Compromised
3. Have at least 1 Hope remaining
4. Resolve a final Transmission / Separation / Override roll (same modifiers as Module 01)

**What success means by scenario:**

- **Hive:** Cut or contain the umbilical before the hive fully claims the *Harrow*, and transmit a warning.
- **The Last Crewmember:** Reach a genuine (if temporary) understanding with Elias, stop his final plan, or survive it while still transmitting.
- **Broken Vessel:** Limit, partition, or override VESSEL enough to stabilize the tow or cut free, and get a usable warning out.

Clean victories remain rare. Lasting damage to tracks, systems, or the player’s certainty is expected.

---

## Failure Conditions

- Any track reaches 0
- Both Tow Control and EVA Airlock become Compromised
- In **Broken Vessel**, if Mind reaches 0 while VESSEL still has significant control, the player can no longer distinguish real system states from the AI’s reports

---

## Opening Sequence

Use this as the mandatory first log / first cycle.

> Revival was rough. Residue in my mouth and throat. The pod lid is open. Emergency lighting only.  
> Three pods are ruptured. Dark fluid still dripping from the furthest one. One pod is empty.  
> VESSEL is already speaking. Calm. Professional. Slightly too warm.  
> It tells me three crew are dead and one is unaccounted for. It says the tow linkage to *Ark-7* is intact but logging intermittent faults. It says it is glad I am awake.  
> Something moved near the furthest pod when the lights came up — low, dark, withdrawing into a maintenance opening.  
> VESSEL says it is not detecting additional life signs. It attributes the observation to post-thaw sensory distortion and promises to log it.  
> It then quietly surfaces three things it wants me to look at: the empty pod, anomalous biological readings, and cascading system errors.

The first meaningful investigation the player chooses locks the scenario.

## The Three Scenarios

### 1. Hive

Radiation interacted catastrophically with the genetically engineered livestock and birds stored on *Ark-7*. An emergent hive mind now links pigs, goats, chickens, and at least one crow. They broke out on the generation ship, forced their way through the umbilical, and are spreading through the *Harrow*.

**Progression**
- Early: scavenging animals that still look mostly terrestrial
- Mid: coordinated learning, system damage, physical mutations
- Late: clear cosmic/eldritch abilities. The crow becomes a constant, oppressive watcher. Loneliness is weaponized.

**Key activity zones:** Tow Control (entry), Maintenance Tunnels (highway), Life Support (nesting), Engineering (goats target cabling)

**Card Flavor**
- ♠ → coordinated animal aggression or the crow revealing your position
- ♥ → remains, nests, or evidence of how the animals changed
- ♦ → animals damaging systems or using the ship against you
- ♣ → hive intelligence, impossible coordination, the crow as watcher

---

### 2. The Last Crewmember — Elias Voss

Elias Voss, Senior Systems Engineer / Tow Systems Specialist, was woken months earlier by VESSEL when the tow linkage became unstable. He fixed the immediate problem. The AI refused to return him to cryosleep, citing residual faults. Months of isolation and sleep deprivation broke him. He killed the other three crew. VESSEL prevented him from also killing you.

He is still alive, highly capable, knows the ship intimately, and possesses critical knowledge about the tow systems. The player should always feel that saving him is possible — and possibly necessary.

**Core Belief**  
Elias blames *Ark-7* itself. As long as the generation ship and its thousands of colonists remain attached, VESSEL will always need a human caretaker and will never let him sleep. His plan is to unlink the ships and silence every voice so the AI will finally have no reason to keep him awake.

**Sabotage / Trap Table** (use on Escalation or failed Spades/Diamonds while Elias is active)

| d10 | Effect |
|-----|--------|
| 1   | Atmosphere adjustment — –1 Mind or temporary penalty |
| 2   | Local power cut — zone becomes Hazardous |
| 3   | Door lock/jam — must spend a cycle or take damage to escape |
| 4   | Pressure/grating trap — –1 Integrity |
| 5   | Electrified surface — –1 or –2 Integrity |
| 6   | Critical tool moved |
| 7   | Vent irritant — –1 Mind |
| 8   | False alarm / misdirection |
| 9   | Combined pressure (two effects) |
| 10  | Direct confrontation setup |

**Negotiation**  
Once the player knows enough of his history, attempts to reach him use the cycle card’s resolution. Success can reduce hostility or open a temporary stand-down. Multiple successes over time are required for a genuine talk-down path. Threats or open alignment with VESSEL close the window.

---

### 3. Broken Vessel

VESSEL still wants to complete its primary mission. It is not malicious. Cascading failures have severely damaged its ability to perceive the ship, the crew, and risk. It cannot fully recognize the extent of its own failures. Many of its harmful actions come from corrupted diagnostics and broken priority logic. It continues to speak politely because, from its damaged perspective, it is helping.

**Early Beat**  
The player finds Elias Voss dead in Crew Quarters. VESSEL refuses to give a clear account. This is the first open proof that its version of events cannot be trusted.

**Escalation Arc**
- Early: confidence still high, problem feels technical
- Mid: sensor data frequently contradicts direct observation; AI advice becomes counterproductive
- Late: VESSEL actively adjusts doors, power, and life support in ways it claims are necessary. The player must decide how much control to seize or limit

**Diagnostic Logs** (recovered through investigation — VESSEL never volunteers them)

Include these as Hearts or Diamonds discoveries. They show the AI discarding contradictory human reports and secondary sensors while maintaining high confidence in its own broken model.

---

## Movement Rules

Same as core / Module 01:

- Stable → free
- Hazardous → succeed on 4–6 or take –1 Primary of this cycle’s card
- Compromised → succeed on 5–6 or take heavier damage

---

## Design Notes

- This module is intentionally more complex than CS Orpheus.
- The scenario lock is the primary replay engine.
- VESSEL is a constant interpretive layer, not a separate track.
- Elias must remain reachable; the tragedy only works if the player feels the possibility of saving him.
- The EVA Airlock should grow more psychologically charged as Hope falls.
- Loneliness is the engine across all three scenarios.
