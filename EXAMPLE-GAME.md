# Example Playthrough

This is a complete short session using the Derelict rules.

---

## Setup

**Character:** Mira Voss, salvage technician. Last remaining member of the *Blackwake* crew after the rest went silent during a recovery job on the derelict research vessel *CS Orpheus*.

**Starting Tracks:**  
Integrity: 6 | Mind: 6 | Hope: 4

**Opening Log:**
> Day 0.  
> I sealed the airlock behind me three hours ago. The *Orpheus* is cold and the emergency lighting is the only thing still running. No response on any frequency. I’m starting the log now in case someone finds this later.  
> Standard procedure. Stay calm. Document everything.

---

## Cycle 1
**Card:** 4 of Diamonds  

Secondary life support in the forward section is fluctuating. Temperature dropping.

**Roll:** 5 → Clean success.  

**Tracks:** Integrity 6 | Mind 6 | Hope 4

**Log:**
> Fixed the regulator in Habitation Ring A. Took longer than it should have. The manual overrides are sticky, like someone forced them recently. Air quality is acceptable again.  
> Still no sign of the crew.

---

## Cycle 2
**Card:** 8 of Hearts  

You find a half-eaten meal and a personal data pad left open on a table in the mess.

**Roll:** 3 → Success with cost.  

**Tracks:** Integrity 6 | Mind 5 | Hope 4

**Log:**
> Found Dr. Keller’s pad. Last entry is just a string of coordinates and the sentence “It’s rewriting the bulkheads from the inside.”  
> The food next to it is only a day old. Maybe less.  
> I turned the pad off. I don’t want to see what else is on it yet.

---

## Cycle 3
**Card:** Jack of Spades  

Something moves in the ceiling above the corridor you’re in. Heavy. Deliberate.

**Roll:** 2 → Failure.  

**Tracks:** Integrity 5 | Mind 4 | Hope 4

**Log:**
> It dropped partway out of a maintenance hatch. Not a person. Not an animal. Looked like the ship’s own insulation had decided to grow limbs. I hit it with the plasma cutter and it pulled back up into the dark.  
> There’s a wet smear on the deck now that wasn’t there before. It’s still warm.

---

## Cycle 4
**Card:** Ace of Clubs  

Major cosmic event. The geometry of the ship changes.

**Roll:** 4 → Success with cost.  

**Tracks:** Integrity 5 | Mind 3 | Hope 3

**Log:**
> The central spine corridor is longer than it was this morning. I timed it. Forty seconds longer. The section markers are in the wrong order. When I turned around, the way I came had become a bulkhead that doesn’t appear on any schematic.  
> I can hear something breathing on the other side of it. Slow. Patient.  
> Hope is starting to feel like a bad joke.

---

## Cycle 5
**Card:** 9 of Diamonds  

A critical power junction fails. Lights die across half the deck.

**Roll:** 1 → Catastrophic.  

**Tracks:** Integrity 3 | Mind 3 | Hope 3

**Log:**
> Primary bus is gone. I tried to jump it and the whole panel arc-flashed. Lost most of the emergency lighting on Decks 2 and 3.  
> Moving by flashlight now. The dark feels thicker here. Like it’s waiting for me to stop moving.

---

## Cycle 6
**Card:** King of Hearts  

Strong human trace. You find the last working security terminal and play the final internal recording.

**Roll:** 2 → Failure.  

**Tracks:** Integrity 3 | Mind 1 | Hope 3

**Log:**
> Watched the last twenty minutes of internal footage.  
> The crew didn’t die all at once. They changed. One by one they stopped speaking in words and started pressing themselves into the walls like they were trying to become part of the ship.  
> Keller was the last one still human. She looked straight into the camera and said, “It’s already inside the ones who are left.”  
> Then she walked into a bulkhead that shouldn’t have opened and the feed cut.  
> I think I’m the only one who still has a name.

---

## Cycle 7
**Card:** 7 of Clubs  

The Unknown presses closer.

**Roll:** 3 → Success with cost.  

**Tracks:** Integrity 3 | Mind 0 | Hope 2

**Log:**
> The walls are breathing in time with me now.  
> I tried to write the coordinates of the nearest exit airlock and my hand wrote something else. Symbols I don’t recognize. When I looked away and looked back, the paper was blank again.  
> I don’t think this place wants me to leave.  
> I don’t think it plans to let me.

*(Mind has reached 0. The character is breaking.)*

---

## Final Cycle
**Card:** 5 of Spades  

Direct threat while trying one last desperate push toward an external airlock.

**Roll:** 1 → Catastrophic.  

**Tracks:** Integrity 1 | Mind 0 | Hope 1

**Final Log:**
> It caught me in the outer ring.  
> I can feel it under my skin now, the same way the walls feel. The flashlight is dying. I’m writing this as fast as I can.  
> If anyone finds this log — do not board the *Orpheus*.  
> It isn’t dead.  
> It’s waiting for the next one.  
>   
> Mira Voss  
> (I think that was my name)

---

## End State

- Mind at 0 → psychological collapse  
- Hope nearly gone  
- Integrity nearly destroyed  
- The final entries shift from professional salvage tech to something quieter and stranger
